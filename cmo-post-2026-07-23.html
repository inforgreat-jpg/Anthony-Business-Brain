Most coaches were trained. They were never tested. That's not an insult. It's a fact about how the industry produces coaches. You sit through a certification program, rack up client hours, and get a credential that says you're qualified to guide someone through a pivot. Nobody in that process asks you to make one yourself. I've been laid off twice. Once after Lehman in 2009, once from a London hedge fund in 2017. I sold almost everything I had at 30, jobless, to start my own fund in the middle of a financial crisis. Fifty people said no before one said yes. That fund got nominated Best New Asian Hedge Fund the year after it almost didn't exist. Years later I bought a group of preschools in Singapore, walked into a team that was 100% women, and had never run anything like it before. Revenue across the group grew more than 10x. I still lost one school outright, because the culture was broken before I signed and no amount of staff changes fixed it. None of that came from a manual. It came from betting on myself in public and finding out, sometimes badly, whether the bet paid off. A clean process is not the same thing as lived judgment. One tells you what should work. The other tells you what happens when it doesn't. I'll take the second every time. Where do you land on this? Change my mind. P.S. I'm not naming names. I'm naming a pattern. You already know which one you hired.
