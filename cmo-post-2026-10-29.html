Nobody checks the hourly rate of their own life. We check the rate on the mortgage. The rate on the car loan. The rate on the savings account, down to the second decimal. Then we hand over most of our waking hours to work and never run the same math on our own time. I did this for years. My last finance job was stable, cushy and paid decently, and I still was not fulfilled. I looked into the mirror and the person looking back at me told me so. More than once. I kept going anyway, because the salary looked so good next to every number I was checking, and I was never checking the one that mattered. Here is what finally moved me. Lying on my death bed, I would regret how I was spending my time. That is the only test I have found that works. Not "is this paying me." Is this paying me what my hours are worth. The most expensive thing you own is not your mortgage. It is every hour spent on work that pays you less than your time is worth. That bill never shows up on a statement, so nobody disputes it. I share this not to make you feel behind. I have been living on borrowed time since I was a kid, and I still let years go by without checking. So here is my question, and I mean it. What is one hour of yours actually worth, and what did you spend it on today? Put your number in the comments. I will reply to every one.
