Full post
Who says a numbers guy cannot roll up his sleeve and look after babies? I used to read my business card as a list of jobs: Credit Suisse M&A, private equity in Hong Kong, my own hedge fund. So when I asked myself whether this was what I wanted or just what I was told to want, I assumed that without the card I had nothing to take anywhere, until I stripped the titles off and found three skills that were never about finance at all. Here is the audit. Name the skill. Name one door it opens. 1. Negotiating. At 27 I sat alone across from the heads of a Chinese state-owned bank. The door: a team of about 50 teachers, all women, where I was the only man and had to earn the right to lead. 2. Hearing no. It took 50 rejections before my hedge fund was seeded. The door: buying preschools when nobody expected a banker to. 3. Reading numbers. A deal is just arithmetic once you can read a balance sheet. The door: growing the group's revenue 10x+ before a strategic sale. I share this not to impress anyone. The takeaway is simple: your title is not what you can do. It is only where you were allowed to do it. You don't need another plan. You need to take one action today. Write your three skills on paper and save this post so you finish before Friday. P.S. Do the audit, then repost this for the person who just got a new title or lost one.
