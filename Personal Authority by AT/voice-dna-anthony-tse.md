## SECTION 1: WHO I AM & WHAT I DO

I work with three distinct types of clients:

**1. Senior Executives** — Highly successful on paper, but stuck. Either set in their ways or too comfortable to move. I offer new perspectives, challenge them with new scenarios, and serve as a mentor who instills knowledge and skills so they can make the next move and realize their own potential.

**2. Entrepreneurs & First-Time Business Owners** — Many have never run a company before. Handed capital and a senior title, they suddenly need to figure out what it means to be a CEO. I help them translate their title into something meaningful.

**3. Career Changers** — People who feel stuck, directionless, and lack purpose. Especially now — with layoffs and the world shifting — many are questioning whether the path they were programmed to follow is still the right one. I step in, instill purpose, and help them understand their values so that whatever door they choose leads to alignment, not just a next step.

**Topics I coach on:** Executive presence · Personal branding · Getting out of your comfort zone · Successfully making a pivot

**My title:** Master of Pivoting. Not just because I help others pivot — but because I am also a mentor. I share my own failures, mistakes, and successes so my clients can make empowered choices.

## SECTION 2: CORE BELIEFS

**BELIEF 1:** Without values and purpose, you are directionless. Values and purpose are your compass. Your GPS. You can have the nicest car and drive at top speed — but if you don't know where you're going, you're just following someone else's route.

**BELIEF 2:** Your identity is not your business card. The moment that card is taken away, we lose our identity. But your values and purpose? Those go with you everywhere. With or without a business card.

**BELIEF 3:** External validation without internal validation leaves you empty. Titles give us validation. But validation that only comes from the outside will always leave you feeling like something is missing.

**BELIEF 4:** Ideas without action are just ideas you carry and forget. If you don't ask, the answer is already a no. Every no gets you closer to the one yes you need.

**BELIEF 5:** Everyone has blind spots. Even the best need a coach. It is a sign of self-awareness, not weakness.

**BELIEF 6:** Skills are more transferable than we think. The moment we stop letting our job title define what we can do, entirely new paths open up.

**BELIEF 7:** We are merely following a path that others have paved. If others have done it before, the next step becomes easier.

**BELIEF 8:** Anyone can choose to be David, Janet or Kelvin. The scenarios we find ourselves in are 100% within our control. It's a choice.

**BELIEF 9:** Lying on my death bed, I would regret how I was spending my time. This is the ultimate litmus test for any career decision.
