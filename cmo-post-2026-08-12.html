You've been successful for years. And yet, something still doesn't feel right. Every conversation about escaping the golden handcuffs hands you the same two doors. Door one: grind harder, chase the next promotion, tell yourself it will feel different this time. Door two: quit, "take the leap," burn the salary and hope the business works before the savings run out. I used to think those were the only two doors too. Then I bought a preschool group while I was still drawing a paycheck. I didn't resign. I didn't gamble the mortgage on a plan I'd never tested. I kept the salary, used it as the floor under me, and hired operators to run the centres while I learned the business from the side of the desk instead of the deep end of it. That decision compounded. Ten times the revenue. Fifty plus staff. An exit I actually chose, on a timeline I actually chose. Here's what nobody selling the quit and chase your dreams version tells you: your salary isn't the thing keeping you trapped. It's the asset you haven't pointed anywhere yet. Ownership doesn't require you to burn the boats. It requires you to buy the boat while you still have a paycheck covering the fuel. If door one and door two both feel wrong, there's a third door. I built a masterclass around exactly how I walked through it. DM me "ETA" and I'll send you the details.
