Lying on my death bed, I would regret how I was spending my time. I knew that years before I did anything about it.

In my last finance job I had stability and a decent salary, and I still filled my calendar the way everyone around me did.

Your calendar is the only record of what you actually value. Not your title. Not your goals deck.

So here is the audit I wish someone had handed me. For one week, tag every block on your calendar with one of three labels:

❤️ Pays me: work someone is paying you for this week
❤️ Builds me: hours that grow a skill, a relationship or an asset you will still own in five years
🫩 Drains me: everything else

Be strict. A meeting you attend out of habit is a Drains block, even when your name is on the invite.

Then add up the hours and look at Builds first. If it is nearly empty, that tells you why the title stopped feeling like enough.

Now ask one question: what is the value of one hour of my time? Put a number on it. Yours, not your employer's.

Then hold the Drains column up against the death bed test. Which of these blocks would I regret on my last day? Those go first.

At 30 I sold almost everything I had to start a hedge fund, and it took 50 rejections before anyone seeded me. I spent my hours where I pointed them. Your calendar does the same, whether you pointed it or not.

You don't need another plan. You need to take one action today.

P.S. Save this, then tag the first block of next week's calendar before you close the tab.

Extend

The audit takes about ten minutes if you export your calendar for the week and colour-code it. Do it on a Sunday night, before Monday has a chance to fill itself in. Then look at the totals before you look at any single block. The totals are what hurt.

Humanise

The day I watched my first child graduate from my own preschool, no meeting on my calendar would have mattered. That is a Builds block I could never have planned in my finance job. I only found it after I started asking where my hours were going.

CTA Amplifier

Which column is biggest on your calendar this week: Pays, Builds or Drains? Drop the word below. And repost this for the one person whose calendar you worry about. They will not ask for the audit, so send it to them anyway.
