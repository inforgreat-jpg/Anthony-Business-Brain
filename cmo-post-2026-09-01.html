Pattern Interrupt hook — selected
My phone rang at 11pm on a Tuesday. David's name flashed on the screen. My first thought was not this can't be good. It was finally. He had just been let go. Called it a restructuring, but we both knew what it meant. Fifteen years of showing up early, saying yes to everything. Gone in one meeting he did not see coming. He was not calling for reassurance. He was calling because the panic would not let him sleep, and he wanted someone who would not tell him it was going to be fine. I did not tell him it was going to be fine. I asked him one question. If your business card disappeared tonight and never came back, who are you tomorrow morning. Silence. Longer than a comfortable pause at that hour. Then he said something he had never said out loud before. He did not know. Twenty years in finance and he genuinely did not know who he was without the title in front of his name. That mattered more than the layoff itself. Most people spend a career building an identity that one email can end. We did not build a plan that night. We built one honest sentence. What he actually wanted, not what looked good on a business card, written down for the first time since business school. That sentence became the compass for every decision after. Not a resume. A compass. Anyone can choose to be David. Most of us are one restructuring meeting away from asking the same question at 11pm. If this is you right now, tell me where you are in the story.
