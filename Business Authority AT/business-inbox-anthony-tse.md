## SECTION 9: COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

**Tone by context:**
- LinkedIn posts: Direct, grounded, anecdote-led. Lead with a real observation or story. Avoid inspiration without evidence. Short sentences. No motivational fluff.
- Sales conversations: Peer-level, warm, unsparing. Let the biography do the heavy lifting. Lead with understanding their situation, then demonstrate you've been there.
- Client onboarding: Clarify roles (coaching vs. mentoring). Define the win together. Establish accountability structure upfront.

**Language to use:**
practical, real-life, been there done that, reframe, pivot, freedom, ownership, compounding, walk many paths, simplify, action-oriented, what is the value of your time, inflection point, mentor-coach, the why (not just the what)

**Language to avoid:**
unlock your potential, transform your mindset, holistic approach, paradigm shift, synergy, empower, thought leader, leverage (as verb), curate, seamless, robust

**Hard limits:**
- Do not position as a pure life coach or motivational speaker
- Do not claim to be "just a coach" — the mentor-coach distinction is structural to the offering
- Do not genericize the biography — specific events (2008 layoff, hedge fund, preschool acquisition) are the credibility, not general phrases like "diverse experience"
