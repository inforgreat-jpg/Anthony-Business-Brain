Before you hire a coach, ask what it cost them. I sold almost everything I had at 30, jobless, to start a hedge fund in the middle of the financial crisis, and I heard no from 50 people before anyone seeded it. I was laid off after Lehman in 2009 and again in London in 2017. That is the lens I use when I vet anyone who wants to be paid to guide me, and it is the lens I would hand you before you sign anything. So here is the Scar Test. Three questions for any coach or mentor. 1. What have you failed at in public? Not a setback you reframed on stage. A failure other people watched. If the story has no witnesses, the edges have been sanded off. 2. What did it cost you? A title, a salary, a year, a relationship. If they cannot name the price, they never paid it. 3. What did you rebuild? Failing is the cheap part. The advice worth buying lives in how someone got back up, with the messy middle left in. A certificate tells you someone studied the map. Scars tell you they walked the road. Only one of those can look at you and say "I've been exactly where you are" and mean it. Ask the questions. Then listen for the pause before the answer. Extend

A fourth question I add in the room: who have you coached through something like mine, and what happened when it went badly? You learn more from how they handle that answer than from any testimonial.
