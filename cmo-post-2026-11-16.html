Topic from Notion
The Title-Stripped Skills Audit: list the 3 skills your job title hides, then name one new door each skill opens.
LinkedIn post
Who says a numbers guy cannot roll up his sleeve and look after babies? I spent years in M&A, private equity and running my own hedge fund, and when I bought my first preschool in Singapore I had never run a classroom in my life, yet the skills that carried me through week one were never printed on my business card, and that is the whole point of today's post. Your job title hides your skills. Here is the audit I ran on myself. 1️⃣ Negotiation Title said: private equity associate. Skill was: sitting alone across from Chinese state-owned bank heads at 27. Door it opens: buying a business from a retiring owner. 2️⃣ Hearing no Title said: fund manager. Skill was: 50 rejections before one investor seeded me. Door it opens: selling your first coaching or consulting engagement. 3️⃣ Reading a team Title said: finance guy. Skill was: leading about 50 people, all women, who knew the work far better than I did. Door it opens: running any operating business. None of these were on a certificate. All of them were transferable. I share this not to impress you. The point is that we stop letting a job title define what we can do, and entirely new paths open up. Take 10 minutes. Strip off your title. Write down the 3 skills it hides. Next to each one, name one door it opens. You don't need another plan. You need to take one action today. Do the audit before Friday, then save this and repost it for the person in your network who is stuck.
